Dimitris Pantazatos

AI & data solutions leader · applied AI and digital transformation

I design and ship production AI systems in enterprise environments — architecture, ML pipelines, cloud deployment and the unglamorous parts that decide whether any of it survives contact with real operations. Ten years across applied AI, data architecture and digital platforms, in Athens.

About

I work at the point where technology, operations and the business have to agree on something. That usually means owning a solution end to end: the architecture, the ML pipelines, the cloud integration, and the release readiness that turns a promising model into a system somebody depends on at 9am on a Monday.

I lead hands-on. I set the standards for quality, security and MLOps, review the code, and mentor engineers across software, data and DevOps — including people who do not report to me. I came to this from economics and informatics rather than from pure engineering, which is probably why I care as much about what a system costs and who has to use it as about how it is built.

Based in Athens. Greek native, English at C2.

My MSc thesis was an introduction to game theory and its applications in economics and negotiations. The prisoner's dilemma is the standard opening example: two players, one move each, and a payoff structure where the rational individual choice leaves both worse off than cooperation would.
